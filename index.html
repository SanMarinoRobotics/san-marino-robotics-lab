Teamwork In Action

Strong Teams Share The Work And Solve Problems Together

1

Share The Robot

Give teammates real opportunities to build, code, test, and drive.

2

Listen First

Understand the idea before responding or changing the design.

3

Use Evidence

Test ideas and let the results guide the team decision.

4

Take Turns

Respect assigned roles and rotate when the coach directs.

5

Solve Conflicts Calmly

Discuss the problem respectfully and ask a coach for help.

6

Support Your Teammates

Give credit, encourage effort, and recover together after setbacks.

When A Rule Is Broken

Correct The Behavior, Learn From It, And Return Ready

1

Reminder

The coach states the expected behavior and redirects you.

2

Quick Coach Talk

You speak privately, listen, and agree on what happens next.

3

Parent Update

The team may pause a related privilege and contact your parent.

4

Team Review

The Head Coach and management decide the next support or condition.

5

Privilege Paused

Practice, driving, Open Practice, or competition participation may pause.

Serious safety, aggression, bullying, or equipment incidents may move directly to a higher step.

Before You Leave

The Team Finishes Cleanup Before The Coach Dismisses Everyone

Batteries and controllers are charging safely
Robots are powered down and stored correctly
Laptops and iPads are returned and accounted for
Parts and tools are sorted into the correct containers
Engineering Notebooks are updated and stored
The field is reset for the next team
Tables, floor, and walkways are clean and clear
Team Members Clean · Coaches Check
Protect Our Equipment

Shared Equipment Stays Safe When Everyone Follows The Same Rules

1

Keep Electronics Off The Floor

Robots, controllers, computers, batteries, motors, and sensors stay on tables or shelves.

2

Carry With Care

Use two hands when needed and follow all charging and testing rules.

3

Return Every Item

Sort parts and return tools and devices to the assigned location.

4

Ask Before Moving Parts

Do not transfer team parts or change shared equipment without approval.

5

Report Damage

Tell a coach immediately about anything missing, broken, hot, or unsafe.

6

Request Parts Correctly

Ask the Head Coach and Ms. Jing when your team needs parts or equipment.

FAQ

Common questions about this season — click to expand.

08 / FAQ

The coaching staff posts a registration form 2–3 weeks before each event. Please complete it by the deadline — late submissions forfeit that event's spot.

Interview times are announced by the event host. Once the coaching staff receives them, they'll be posted in Announcements and sent directly to the parents and members of the competing teams.

Registration fees are covered by the club. Travel and lodging costs (for out-of-town championships) are billed separately in advance, with amounts listed in Announcements.

Each event includes Teamwork Matches and the Skills Challenge, scored and ranked together. Official rules follow the current season's Game Manual, which coaches cover in Workshops.

Uniforms are ordered through the club — new members are measured after joining. Competition toolboxes and common parts are available at the lab; members should bring a basic screwdriver set.

Please notify the coaching staff in advance so lineups and transportation can be adjusted. For day-of emergencies, call the lead coach directly.

The Results and Announcements sections on this page are updated continuously, and important notices are also posted in the team chat.
